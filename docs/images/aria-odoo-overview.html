ARIA+Odoo: who does what

ARIA finds and engages the right people. Odoo Recruitment runs the hiring. They work as one pipeline, with one clear hand-off in the middle.

ARIA → Odoo Odoo → ARIA
A

ARIA

Find & engage
  • Finds people who did not applySearches public profiles (web, GitHub, LinkedIn) for each open position
  • Scores every profileA /100 fit against the brief, with the evidence behind it
  • Makes the first contactEmail and LinkedIn messages, each one approved by a recruiter
  • Follows the repliesSpots who is interested, keeps a talent pool for later needs
Owns: prospects, before anyone has applied.
1

A job opensOdoo → ARIA

Import an open Odoo job position: it becomes the campaign brief.

2

“Send to Odoo”ARIA → Odoo

A recruiter picks a candidate: ARIA creates the applicant, with the score and evidence as a note.

3

Stage updates, liveOdoo → ARIA

Interview, offer, hired or refused in Odoo: ARIA updates the candidate at once.

4

GDPR erasureARIA → Odoo

Erase a candidate in ARIA and the applicant is deleted in Odoo (hired people stay with HR).

O

Odoo Recruitment

Hire & manage
  • Job positionsThe approved need: what to hire, how many, who recruits
  • Applications pipelineEvery applicant and their stage, in one place for HR
  • Interviews, offers, contractsScheduling, decisions and refusal reasons
  • Hire and onboardThe hired applicant becomes an employee record
Owns: applications and hires, the system of record.
