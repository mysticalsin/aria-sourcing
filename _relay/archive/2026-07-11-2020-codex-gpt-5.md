## Watch out

- A remount can hide a legacy root cluster; the guard prevents silent initialization but does not replace preserved-clone inspection.
- `/var/lib/postgresql` is also postgres HOME. Image-level history suppression and strict regular-file cleanup must remain together.
- Never scale `aria-mantu-db` above one Machine in this non-replicated topology.
- A stopped database can cause sibling `.internal` name resolution failures; prove PostgreSQL first when diagnosing Auth.
- Fly `--stage` records pending mutations. A timeout or signal can leave activation outcome ambiguous; do not invent rollback success.
- Mutating GoTrue/PostgREST calls can commit before a lost response. Preserve ambiguous identities for reconciliation.
- GitHub reruns expose a distinct triggering actor. Checking only the initial actor permits self-approval.
- Removing a previous encryption key can permanently orphan ciphertext.
- The final accepted receipt must bind the exact uploaded evidence artifact ID and digest.
- Local Fly-domain connection timeouts can be a route problem. They do not substitute for provider inventory and protected live acceptance.
- Do not broaden Gitleaks allowlists. Current exceptions are fingerprint- or line-specific.
- Do not push `_relay`, incidents, agent state, raw logs, screenshots, or local binaries into a public release.
- Do not mark the whole product production-ready while the broader campaign correctness findings remain open.
