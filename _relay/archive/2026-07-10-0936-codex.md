## Decisions made (don't relitigate)
- `touchCount` must be derived from joined `state.outreach` messages, not from `candidate.outreachHistory.length`.
- Dry-run history entries are not wins and do not count as real touches.
- `outreachChannel` means the most recent real completed send in the joined history.
- `timeToBookMs` is `booking.createdAt - earliest real send time`; absent real sends produce `null`.
- Win derivation is best-effort and cannot block booking creation.
- Winlog export remains inside the browser session because WinRecord contains candidate PII.
